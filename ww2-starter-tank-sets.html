WW2 Starter Tank Sets

Compare starter sets and game-ready packs when you want grouped contents instead of selecting every miniature separately.

Starter sets are useful for browsing by army. Game-ready packs are useful when you want fixed-scale scenario contents.

Nation Starter Sets

German, Soviet, and US starter sets are the simplest way to begin a force. They keep the browsing decision focused on faction and broad vehicle mix.

Game-Ready Packs

Hellcat Tankers and Lone Sherman packs are more specific: they are built around fixed-scale tabletop play and pack contents rather than a broad army starter role.
