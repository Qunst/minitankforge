How to Request or Buy MiniTankForge Miniatures

Build a non-binding Request Basket, or continue to Etsy checkout
Before you start

A Request Basket is not a checkout

You can choose products and send a complete order request through MiniTankForge, but no payment is taken on this website. The request is not a confirmed order until I review the details, confirm the final total, and payment is completed.

Direct request: build and send your basket, then confirm shipping and payment by email.
Etsy: open the product listing and complete checkout through Etsy.
Browse Tanks Browse Sets Open Request Basket
Step 1

Choose products and options

Open a tank or set page and select the scale, finish, pack size or set option, and quantity you want. The displayed price changes with your selection.

Step 2

Add everything to your Request Basket

Select Add to Request Basket on each product page. You can combine different tanks and sets, adjust quantities, remove items, or return to a product page to change options.

Step 3

Review and send the request

Check every model and option, then enter your name, email, country, and any useful delivery or deadline notes. Sending the basket does not take payment or create a confirmed order.

Step 4

Receive your email copy

An automatic email is sent to the address you entered. It includes your request ID, selected items, options, quantities, and estimated product subtotal. This confirms receipt—not final availability, shipping, or acceptance of the order.

If it does not appear shortly, check your spam folder and save the request ID shown on the page.

After you send

I confirm the final details personally

I will review availability, production time, shipping, and your final total, then reply by email. Once everything is agreed, payment can be completed by PayPal invoice or bank transfer. The order is confirmed after payment.

Send request → receive automatic copy → confirm shipping and final total → complete payment
Price information

What the estimated subtotal includes

The Request Basket uses the product prices shown on MiniTankForge. No tax or Etsy fees are added there. Shipping is not included and will be calculated and confirmed by email before payment.

Alternative

Buy through Etsy

If you prefer marketplace checkout, use the Etsy button on a product page and choose the same scale, finish, pack size, or set option there. Payment and checkout are completed on Etsy.

Browse here → open the Etsy listing → choose the same options → complete Etsy checkout
