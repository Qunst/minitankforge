Model notes

The Marder III Ausf. H is a German tank destroyer miniature with a tall open fighting compartment and long anti-tank gun. It fits naturally into mid-war Eastern Front, North African, and mobile anti-armor formations beside Panzer 38(t), Panzer III, and Panzer IV vehicles.

Historical note

The Marder III Ausf. H mounted the 7.5 cm PaK 40 anti-tank gun on the Panzer 38(t) chassis in an open-topped fighting compartment.
