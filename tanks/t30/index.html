Model notes

The T30 is an American prototype heavy tank that adds a bigger-gun branch of the T29 family for experimental and alternate-history forces. It works well beside the T29, T34 Heavy Tank, and T28/T95 when you want a full spread of late American heavy designs.

Historical note

The T30 used the same heavy chassis family as the T29 but mounted a much larger 155 mm gun for heavy assault work.
