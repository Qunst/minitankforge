Model notes

The E-75 gives the E-series concept a heavier tabletop anchor, pairing late-war German styling with a more imposing battlefield role. It is useful for alternate-history scenarios, experimental platoons, or collections that already include the E-50 and want a heavier partner.

Historical note

The E-75 was intended as a standard heavy tank partner to the E-50 in the same late-war E-series program.
