Model notes

The Tiger II is a late-war German heavy tank with a large, angular profile that makes it a clear tabletop threat. It is a strong centerpiece for defensive scenarios, elite armor groups, and matchups against stronger late-war Allied or Soviet vehicles.

Historical note

The Tiger II combined the sloped armor concept of the Panther with the heavy-gun philosophy of the Tiger I.
