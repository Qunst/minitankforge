Model notes

The M18 Hellcat is a fast-looking American tank destroyer that suits mobile ambushes, reconnaissance-heavy scenarios, and hit-and-run tabletop play. Its open-topped profile and compact hull give Allied forces a different visual rhythm from standard Sherman-based armor.

Historical note

The Hellcat was one of the fastest tracked armored vehicles fielded during the war.
