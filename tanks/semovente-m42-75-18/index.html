Model notes

The Semovente M42 75/18 is an Italian self-propelled gun miniature for Mediterranean, Italian campaign, and late-war Axis forces. Its low casemate and compact hull provide a useful assault-gun silhouette beside M13/40 and M14/41 tanks.

Historical note

The Semovente M42 75/18 paired the short 75 mm howitzer with the later M15/42-family chassis in a low, enclosed fighting compartment.
