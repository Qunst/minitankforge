Model notes

The IS-7 gives Soviet collections a large late-1940s heavy tank prototype with a broad hull, rounded turret, and long-gun profile. It is useful for post-war what-if scenarios, prototype lineups, and players who want a Soviet super-heavy centerpiece beside IS-series tanks.

Historical note

The IS-7 was a late-1940s Soviet heavy tank prototype with a low hull, rounded turret, and very heavy armor.
