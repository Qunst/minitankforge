Model notes

The Bishop adds early British self-propelled artillery to North African and Mediterranean scenarios. Its unusually tall fighting compartment makes it a characterful support vehicle beside Valentines, Matildas, trucks, and towed artillery.

Historical note

The Bishop mounted a 25-pounder gun-howitzer in a tall fixed superstructure on the Valentine tank chassis.
