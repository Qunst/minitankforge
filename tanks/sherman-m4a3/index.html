Model notes

The Sherman M4A3 is a dependable Allied workhorse for USA forces, ideal for building platoons, scenario groups, and mixed support columns. Its recognizable shape makes it an easy anchor model for tabletop games, dioramas, and larger resin armor collections.

Historical note

Produced in huge numbers, the Sherman became the backbone of Allied armored forces.
