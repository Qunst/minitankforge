Model notes

The E-100 is a German super-heavy what-if tank miniature for late-war and alternate-history tabletop forces. Its enormous hull gives it immediate presence beside Panthers, Tigers, Maus-class vehicles, and other E-series designs, making it a strong centerpiece for experimental armor scenarios.

Historical note

The E-100 is one of Germany's most famous unfinished super-heavy tank projects, ideal for what-if late-war tables.
