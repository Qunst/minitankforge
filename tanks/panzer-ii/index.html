Ask about this tank

Model notes

The Panzer II is an early-war German light tank miniature for Poland, France, North Africa, and reconnaissance-themed tabletop forces. It fills the useful size and capability gap between the tiny Panzer I and larger Panzer III or Panzer IV vehicles.

Historical note

Although conceived as a temporary design, the Panzer II became an important German light tank in the opening campaigns of WW2 and later continued in reconnaissance roles.
