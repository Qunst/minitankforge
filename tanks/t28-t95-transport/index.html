Model notes

This T28/T95 model shows the American super-heavy prototype in its narrower transport configuration with the outer track units removed. It is useful as a display counterpart to the full combat configuration and for rail, recovery, or deployment scenes.

Historical note

The T28/T95 could remove its outer track units and join them into a towable assembly to reduce its width for transport.
