Model notes

The M60A1 brings the catalog into Cold War armor with a recognizable American main battle tank profile. It is useful for players who want post-war scenarios, modernizing USA forces, or a heavier vehicle that visually differs from WW2 designs.

Historical note

The M60A1 introduced a redesigned turret and became a long-serving Cold War main battle tank.
