Model notes

The M3 Half-track is a support vehicle that helps tabletop forces feel like complete formations rather than only tanks. It is useful for infantry transport, scenario objectives, convoy tables, and game packs that need vehicles with a different battlefield job.

Historical note

The M3 half-track moved infantry and support teams while keeping better cross-country mobility than a standard truck.
