Model notes

The Opel Blitz is a German truck model for supply columns, convoy scenarios, rear-area objectives, and support-heavy tables. It adds practical non-tank detail to a collection and helps make armored forces feel connected to the logistics behind them.

Historical note

The Opel Blitz was one of Germany's most important wartime trucks, used for transport, supply, and support roles.
