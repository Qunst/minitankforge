Model notes

The Renault R35 is a compact French light tank miniature for the 1940 campaign, infantry-support forces, and early-war collections. Its rounded cast hull and tiny one-man turret make it visibly different from the larger Somua S35 and Char B1 bis.

Historical note

The Renault R35 was a small, well-armored infantry-support tank intended to replace the Renault FT in French service.
