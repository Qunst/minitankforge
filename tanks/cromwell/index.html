Model notes

The Cromwell IV is a fast British medium tank for Normandy, reconnaissance, and late-war Commonwealth forces. Its low hull, boxy turret, and five large road wheels give it a distinctly British profile beside Shermans, Churchills, and German medium tanks.

Historical note

The Cromwell combined the powerful Meteor engine with excellent mobility, making it one of Britain's fastest wartime cruiser tanks.
