Model notes

The Somua S40 is a French early-war prototype medium tank for 1940 what-if forces, campaign extensions, and players who want French armor beyond the familiar S35. Its rounded hull and compact turret keep the Somua family look while adding a rarer development-branch vehicle.

Historical note

The Somua S40 was planned as an improved development of the S35, but France fell before it could become a normal production tank.
