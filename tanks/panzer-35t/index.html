Model notes

The Panzer 35(t) is a small early-war German light tank that helps fill out invasion-era and early campaign forces. Its compact profile makes it useful for showing how different early-war armor looked compared with later German vehicles.

Historical note

The Panzer 35(t) was a Czech-built light tank pressed into German service for early-war campaigns.
