Model notes

The Type 97 Chi-Ha is a key Japanese medium tank for Pacific and early-war tabletop collections. It works well as the central armored vehicle for Japanese forces, especially when paired with lighter Ha-Go tanks and infantry-heavy scenarios.

Historical note

The Chi-Ha was Japan's most important medium tank design of the war and appeared in several improved variants.
