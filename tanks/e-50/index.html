Model notes

The E-50 is a late-war German what-if medium tank that works well for players building E-series or alternate-history formations. It has enough Panther-like presence to feel plausible on the table while still offering something different from the standard German lineup.

Historical note

The E-50 was planned as a standardized German medium tank concept, making it a strong E-series what-if model.
