Model notes

The T-34/85 is a late-war Soviet medium tank miniature with the familiar T-34 hull and a stronger 85 mm gun turret. It is one of the best choices for Eastern Front tabletop forces, Soviet spearheads, Berlin-era scenarios, and collections that need a recognizable Red Army workhorse.

Historical note

The T-34/85 added a larger turret and 85 mm gun, giving the classic Soviet medium tank a stronger late-war presence.
