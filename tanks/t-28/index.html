Model notes

The T-28 is a distinctive Soviet medium tank for early-war and interwar-inspired scenarios, with a multi-turret layout that stands apart from later designs. It is a strong visual choice for players who want Soviet forces that feel older, heavier, and less standardized.

Historical note

The Soviet T-28 was an interwar multi-turret medium tank designed to support infantry and break fortified positions.
