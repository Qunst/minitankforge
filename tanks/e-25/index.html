Model notes

The E-25 is a compact what-if German tank destroyer with a low, aggressive profile that suits ambush scenarios and late-war experimental forces. It is a good choice when you want something smaller than the heavy tank destroyers but still visually distinct from standard armor.

Historical note

The E-25 was planned as a fast, low-profile tank destroyer in Germany's late-war E-series concept family.
