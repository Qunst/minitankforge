Model notes

The M30 Cargo Carrier is an American tracked ammunition-carrier miniature for M12 batteries, logistics columns, resupply objectives, and late-war support formations. Its shared chassis makes it an ideal companion to the M12 while its open cargo arrangement clearly identifies its support role.

Historical note

The M30 carried ammunition and gun crews for the M12, using a closely related chassis with cargo stowage in place of the 155 mm gun.
