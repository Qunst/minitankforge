Model notes

The Jagdpanzer IV is a low-profile German tank destroyer that fits neatly into late-war formations built around Panzer IV chassis vehicles. It is especially useful for defensive scenarios, mixed armor groups, and tables where silhouette variety matters.

Historical note

The Jagdpanzer IV brought a low silhouette and sloped armor to the Panzer IV chassis in a dedicated anti-tank role.
