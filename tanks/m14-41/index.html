Model notes

The M14/41 works well as a companion to the M13/40 for Italian armored groups, desert scenarios, and compact WW2 tabletop forces. It keeps the recognizable Italian medium-tank silhouette while giving Axis collections a little more variety than German armor alone.

Historical note

The M14/41 was an improved Italian medium tank derived from the M13/40, with engine and detail changes.
