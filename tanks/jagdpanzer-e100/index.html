Model notes

The Jagdpanzer E 100 is a huge German what-if tank destroyer miniature for late-war, alternate-history, and super-heavy armor collections. Its oversized casemate and long-gun profile make it a dramatic scenario objective, table centerpiece, or final-stage experimental vehicle beside the Maus and E-100.

Historical note

The Jagdpanzer E 100 was a paper-project casemate tank destroyer built around the idea of mounting extreme firepower on the E-100 chassis.
