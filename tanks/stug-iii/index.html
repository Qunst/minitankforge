Model notes

The StuG III is a German assault gun that fits well into late-war support roles, defensive formations, and compact tabletop armor groups. Its low casemate shape and Panzer III-based chassis make it a useful companion to Panzer III, Panzer IV, and German tank destroyer models while still adding a different battlefield purpose.

Historical note

The StuG III was built on the Panzer III chassis and became Germany's most widely produced fully tracked armored fighting vehicle of the war.
