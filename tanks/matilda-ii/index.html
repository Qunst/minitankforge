Model notes

The Matilda II is a distinctive British infantry tank for France 1940, the Western Desert, and Commonwealth forces. Its armored side skirts and compact turret make it immediately recognizable beside faster cruiser tanks and lighter reconnaissance vehicles.

Historical note

The Matilda II earned a formidable early-war reputation from its heavy armor, especially during the fighting in North Africa.
