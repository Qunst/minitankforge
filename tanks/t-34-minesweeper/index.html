Model notes

The T-34 Minesweeper adds engineering character to Soviet forces, making it useful for minefield, assault, and prepared-defense scenarios. It is a strong choice when you want the table to tell a mission story rather than only show tank-on-tank fighting.

Historical note

Mine-roller T-34 variants helped armored formations push through mined approaches and prepared defenses.
