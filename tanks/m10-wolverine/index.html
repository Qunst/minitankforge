Model notes

The M10 Wolverine adds a dedicated American tank destroyer to Allied forces, with an open turret and a more specialized role than the Sherman. It is useful for ambush scenarios, mobile anti-armor screens, and mixed USA armored groups.

Historical note

The M10 was one of the most common Allied tank destroyers, using an open-topped turret and a 3-inch gun.
