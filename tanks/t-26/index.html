Model notes

The T-26 is a useful early-war Soviet light tank for infantry support, Spanish Civil War inspired setups, and the opening years of WW2. Its compact size makes it a natural companion to T-28s, BA armored cars, and other older Soviet vehicles.

Historical note

The T-26 was one of the most numerous Soviet light tanks before and during the early stages of WW2.
