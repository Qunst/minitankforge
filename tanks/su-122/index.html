Model notes

The SU-122 gives Soviet forces a close-support self-propelled gun with a heavier howitzer role than the SU-76. It is useful for assault scenarios, infantry support themes, and collections that show the range of T-34-based Soviet vehicles.

Historical note

The SU-122 used a 122 mm howitzer on a T-34-based chassis for close support and assault-gun work.
