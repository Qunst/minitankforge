Browse heavy and super-heavy tank miniatures for stronger table presence and late-war scenario focus.

Heavy tanks are best used as centerpiece models, scenario anchors, or small groups beside medium tanks and support vehicles.

Heavy Tank Choices

Tiger I, Tiger II, IS-series tanks, KV-2, and Pershing give forces a different visual weight than medium tanks. German super-heavy designs add experimental and alternate-history variety.

How To Use Them

A heavy tank works well as one centerpiece in a compact group. If you want more balanced play, pair it with medium tanks, tank destroyers, or lighter reconnaissance vehicles.
