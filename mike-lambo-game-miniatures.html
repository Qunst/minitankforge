Game accessory guide

Miniatures for Mike Lambo Solo Wargames

Browse fixed-scale miniature accessory packs for players who want physical pieces alongside Mike Lambo solo book wargames.

These are unofficial, fan-made 3D printed accessories compatible with the named games. They are not the game books, printed maps, rules, or official Mike Lambo products.

Why These Packs Are Different

The normal armor sets are broad vehicle groups. These game packs are more specific: they are built around the pieces a player may want when replacing or supplementing paper counters with small physical miniatures.

Fixed Game Scales

Hellcat Tankers uses a compact 1:285 pack. The Lone Sherman packs use 1:250, giving the Sherman-centered games a little more table presence while staying practical for solo play.

Good For

Solo wargame players who like the clarity and table feel of physical pieces, especially when scenarios involve several vehicles, infantry, guns, or support units.

Project Note

The game-set side of MiniTankForge grew after discovering Mike Lambo's games, and the tanks have appeared in videos connected to those games. There is more of that background on the About page.

Not Included

These packs do not include game rules, books, maps, dice, or official counters. Use them as miniature accessories alongside the games you already own or plan to buy separately.
