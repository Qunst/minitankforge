## Current Follower-Growth Plan

The goal is qualified followers: tank enthusiasts, historical wargamers,
miniature painters, and solo-game players.

- Publish two strong Reels and one carousel per week when enough good material
  exists. Quality and repeatable formats matter more than daily filler.
- Use Trial Reels for unusual concepts intended to reach non-followers.
- Attempt one relevant Instagram Collab every two weeks.
- Use specific calls to action such as `Follow for the next field test` or
  `Which tank should receive this treatment next?`
- Spend 10-15 minutes most days leaving genuine comments on a small number of
  relevant posts and replying to all genuine comments.
- Track non-follower reach, shares, average watch time, profile visits, and
  follows after 72 hours. Repeat formats that generate shares and follows, not
  merely likes.
- Never buy followers, automate engagement, mass-follow accounts, or use an
  unfollow-after-follow routine.

Priority accounts for selective following and engagement were recorded in the
portable handoff. The highest-value starting group is Wargames Illustrated,
Wargames Soldiers & Strategy, Beyond Solitaire, Headlines from a Solo Player,
OnTableTop, Reaction Games Models, TankHistoria, and relevant miniature painters.
