## 4. Full description

```text
QF 6-pounder British 57 mm anti-tank gun miniature for WW2 tabletop games, British and Commonwealth forces, defensive positions, ambushes, and artillery batteries. This model represents the towed Ordnance QF 6-pounder 7 cwt on an integrated scenic base.

This listing is for the QF 6-pounder specifically, not the British 2-pounder, 17-pounder, or a tank-mounted gun.

Available scales:
1:160, 1:180, 1:200, 1:250, and 1:285.

Available finishes:
Unpainted - cleaned resin print, ready for primer and painting.
Base coated - simple nation-appropriate base coat, useful if you want a faster table-ready starting point.

Pack sizes:
1 artillery piece, 5 artillery pieces, or 10 artillery pieces.

The 6-pounder first saw action in North Africa in April 1942. It gave British forces a much stronger anti-tank weapon than the earlier 2-pounder and remained in British infantry anti-tank service for the rest of the war. It works well in desert-war, Normandy, Italy, north-west Europe, and Commonwealth tabletop forces.

This standalone model is also the gun piece included in my unofficial Lone Panther accessory pack.

These are small 3D printed resin miniatures. Some light cleanup may be needed before painting. Details and thicknesses vary by scale, and very small parts should be handled carefully.

Printed in ABS-like resin, which is tougher and less brittle than standard resin, but still should be treated as a miniature model rather than a toy.

You can also browse more tanks, finish examples, and scale information on my MiniTankForge site:
https://minitankforge.com
```
