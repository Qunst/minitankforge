## Launch Checklist

Do these before outreach:

1. Create the account with the best available handle.
2. Add the profile image.
3. Add the display name, bio, and tracked website link.
4. Switch to professional/business.
5. Upload the 9 launch posts.
6. Check the grid on mobile after upload.
7. Follow only a small starter batch: existing customers, relevant hobby accounts, and creators you genuinely want to interact with.
8. Reply to any first comments before posting elsewhere.

## First Week After Launch

Day 1:

- Create account.
- Seed all 9 posts.
- Follow 10 to 20 highly relevant accounts.

Day 2:

- Post one Story with the scale guide link.
- Comment usefully on 5 relevant posts. Do not pitch.

Day 3:

- Reuse `01-scale-lineup.jpg` as the first Pinterest pin.
- Reuse `07-finish-comparison.jpg` as the second Pinterest pin.

Day 4:

- Post a Reel or Story using the catalog promo video.
- Rotate profile link to the scale guide if that is the current focus.

Day 5:

- Make one useful community post using the scale guide, only where links are allowed.

Day 6:

- Review saves, profile visits, website clicks, and follows.
- Pick the next 3 posts based on whichever launch post gets the most signal.

Day 7:

- Record stats in the weekly marketing dashboard.

## Notes

- Do not say "buy cheaper direct" or try to move Etsy-started buyers away from Etsy.
- For game-related packs, use "unofficial accessory pack" language.
- Keep every post tied to one job and one destination.
- Reuse launch assets on Pinterest after the Instagram grid is live.
