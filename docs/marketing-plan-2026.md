# MiniTankForge Marketing Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-07

This plan is built for two linked goals:

1. Increase Etsy sales for MiniTankForge tank products.
2. Drive more qualified hobby traffic to https://minitankforge.com.

The working rule is simple: Etsy is the checkout engine, MiniTankForge.com is the catalog, proof, scale guide, and repeat-discovery engine. Do not use Etsy messages, Etsy listings, QR codes, or Etsy buyer conversations to move an Etsy-started transaction off Etsy. Etsy prohibits actions that encourage buyers to purchase an Etsy item through another venue. Use the site for browsing, scale information, finish examples, and catalog discovery, then send Etsy buyers back to Etsy to purchase.

## Current Position

Known strengths:

- Etsy shop: Quali3DPrints, Samobor, Croatia.
- Etsy proof: Star Seller, 649 sales, 150 reviews, 4.9 rating, 4 years on Etsy.
- Strong current tank signals: Lone Sherman Pack, Hellcat Tankers Pack, German/US/USSR WW2 tank listings.
- Site proof: individual tank pages, set pages, scale comparison, finish guide, gallery, reviews, FAQ, request page, schema markup, direct Etsy links.
- Catalog depth: dozens of single tanks, multiple scale options, base coat and unpainted finish options, starter sets, game-ready packs, nation guides, scale guides.

Main problem:

The Etsy shop still reads as a mixed 3D print shop, while MiniTankForge.com reads as a focused tank brand. The marketing work must make the tank brand dominate every buyer touchpoint.

Primary buyer segments:

- Board game players who want replacement or upgrade pieces for compact WW2 games.
- Micro armor and hex-and-counter players who need 1:250 or 1:285 vehicles.
- Wargamers building German, Soviet, US, UK, Japanese, Italian, or prototype forces.
- Gift buyers shopping for a tabletop gamer, WW2 hobbyist, or model collector.
- Repeat buyers who already trust print quality and want more vehicles.

## 90-Day Sales Target

Use these concrete targets for the first 90 days:

- Etsy: increase tank-related sales by 30 percent versus the previous 90 days.
- Site: reach 500 qualified monthly visits from organic/social/referral traffic.
- Etsy conversion: improve top 10 tank listings before running meaningful ad spend.
- Product mix: make sets and game packs at least 50 percent of promoted listings.
- Repeat buyers: include a catalog QR insert in 100 percent of Etsy tank orders.

Track progress every Monday. Do not wait for perfect data.

## Strategy

The plan has six channels:

1. Etsy search optimization: make each listing match buyer searches better.
2. Etsy conversion optimization: improve photos, titles, descriptions, sections, and offers.
3. Website SEO: turn MiniTankForge.com into the best browsing and comparison layer.
4. Content distribution: publish repeatable photos/videos that show scale, detail, and use.
5. Community outreach: get in front of tabletop, board game, and micro armor audiences.
6. Retention: turn every order into the next catalog visit and Etsy repeat purchase.

The top product focus for the first 90 days:

- Game-ready packs: Hellcat Tankers Pack, Lone Sherman Pack, Lone Sherman Pacific Pack, Undaunted Reinforcements Normandy Tank Pack if/when the Etsy listing is ready.
- Starter sets: German Starter Set, USSR Starter Set, US Starter Set.
- High-recognition singles: Sherman M4A3, Tiger I, Tiger II, Panther, T-34, T-34/85, Panzer IV, M18 Hellcat, M10 Wolverine.
- High-curiosity singles: Maus, E-100, E-75, Jagdpanzer E 100, T29, T30, T34 Heavy Tank, KV-5, A39 Tortoise.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not advertise unoptimized listings.
- Do not spend ads on single EUR 2.50 items unless the listing has pack-size variations that push order value up.
- Do not use Etsy to tell buyers to buy direct by PayPal.
- Do not use "official", "licensed", or game/publisher names in a way that implies endorsement.
- Do not keep the Etsy storefront visually mixed during the 90-day push. Tanks must dominate featured items, announcement copy, photos, and shop sections.
- Do not renew listings just for tiny recency boosts. Etsy itself says improving tags and conversion is better than relying on renewals.

### Attribute Checklist

For every listing, fill every relevant Etsy attribute:

- Category: choose the most specific toy/model/miniature category Etsy provides.
- Material: Resin.
- Scale: use one allowed Etsy scale attribute, usually 1:160, while the description and variations list all scales.
- Color: match finish options where possible.
- Occasion: leave blank unless the item is truly made for that occasion.
- Personalization: off unless it is a real custom option.
- Handmade category: make sure the listing truthfully fits Etsy's Creativity Standards.

For 3D printed items, keep design/originality documentation ready. Etsy's current Creativity Standards say items produced using computerized tools, including 3D printers, must be based on the seller's original design. Keep a folder with source/model notes, modification notes, screenshots, and your own product photos.

## Product And Offer Strategy

### Make Sets The Main Sales Engine

Sets have better order value, easier ad economics, and clearer buyer decisions than one EUR 2.50 tank.

Promote this ladder:

1. Game pack: "I need pieces for this game."
2. Starter set: "I want a complete German/Soviet/US force."
3. Iconic single tank: "I want this specific tank."
4. Experimental single tank: "I want something unusual."
5. Custom request: "I want a vehicle not listed."

### Add Three Etsy Bundle Listings

Create these listings if they do not already exist:

1. Eastern Front Starter Bundle:
   - German Starter Set + USSR Starter Set.
   - Title: `Eastern Front WW2 Tank Bundle - German and Soviet 3D Printed Miniatures`
   - Use case: two opposing starter forces.

2. Normandy Armor Bundle:
   - US Starter Set + German Starter Set, or US tank set + German tank set.
   - Title: `Normandy WW2 Tank Bundle - US and German 3D Printed Tabletop Armor`
   - Use case: Allied vs German scenario start.

3. Heavy Armor Showcase Bundle:
   - Tiger I, Tiger II, IS-2, Pershing, Maus, T29 or Tortoise.
   - Title: `WW2 Heavy Tank Miniature Bundle - 3D Printed Tabletop Armor Set`
   - Use case: collectors and gift buyers.

Price bundles so the buyer sees a reason to buy the set instead of picking singles. A 10 to 15 percent bundle advantage is enough.

### Push Pack Sizes On Single Tanks

For single tanks, keep 1 tank, 5 tanks, and 10 tanks. In the description, explicitly say:

```text
Choose 1 tank for a sample or character vehicle, 5 tanks for a small tabletop group, or 10 tanks for a larger formation.
```

This raises average order value without confusing the listing.

### Ad Review Rules

Every Friday, record:

```text
Listing
Ad spend
Ad clicks
Ad revenue
Orders
Favorites
Click-through rate
Conversion rate
Search terms if available
Decision: keep / fix / pause
```

Decision rules:

- Keep: any listing with profitable sales or repeated favorites/carts and improving conversion.
- Fix: 20 or more ad clicks, no order, but favorites occur. Improve photo 1 and description.
- Pause: 30 or more ad clicks, no favorites, no orders.
- Pause: ad spend reaches expected gross profit from one average order and no sale occurs.
- Scale: two profitable weeks in a row. Raise budget by EUR 2/day, not more.

Target ad economics:

- Sets and bundles: acceptable if ad spend is 15 to 25 percent of ad revenue during testing.
- Single tanks: acceptable only if pack-size purchases keep average order value high.
