## Quick reference from the 1:250 master

- 1:100 — **250%**
- 1:144 — **174%**
- 1:160 — **156%**
- 1:180 — **139%**
- 1:200 — **125%**
- 1:250 — **100%**
- 1:285 — **88%**

The values are rounded to the nearest whole percent. For the best consistency, always scale directly from the original file's scale rather than applying several rounded conversions in sequence.

Exact formula: **source denominator ÷ target denominator × 100**.
