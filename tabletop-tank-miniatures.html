Choosing for Play

For large battles and compact layouts, smaller scales such as 1:285 and 1:250 keep more vehicles on the table. For display presence, larger scale options make individual models easier to inspect.

Choosing by Collection Style

Start with individual tanks if you know exactly what you want. Start with a set if you want a force, scenario pack, or nation group without choosing every vehicle separately.
