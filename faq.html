Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Quick answers about Request Baskets, confirmation emails, prices, payment, Etsy, and product options.

Can I send an order request directly from this site?

Yes. Choose products and options, add them to the Request Basket, and send a non-binding order request. No payment is taken on MiniTankForge, and the request is not a confirmed order until I review the details, confirm the final total, and payment is completed.

What happens after I send a Request Basket?

An automatic email sends you a copy with the request ID, selected products, options, quantities, and estimated product subtotal. I then review availability, production time, shipping, and the final total and reply personally by email.

Does the automatic confirmation email mean my order is confirmed?

No. The automatic email confirms only that your request was received. Availability, production time, shipping, and the final total still need to be reviewed and confirmed.

What does the estimated product subtotal include?

It uses the product prices shown on MiniTankForge. No tax or Etsy fees are added to the Request Basket. Shipping is not included and will be calculated and confirmed by email.

How can I pay after the details are confirmed?

After availability, shipping, and the final total are agreed, payment can be completed by PayPal invoice or bank transfer. The order is confirmed after payment.

Is Etsy still an option?

Yes. You can still use Etsy if you prefer marketplace checkout. This site simply makes browsing, comparing, and understanding the products easier first.

Is a single tank page for one tank or for multiple tanks?

A single tank page is for one tank unless the page clearly states otherwise.

Can I choose scale and finish?

Yes. Available scale and finish options are shown while browsing and again on the product pages.

What is the difference between unpainted and base coat?

Unpainted models arrive without primer or paint for hobbyists who want to do their own finishing. Base coat models receive a colored primer layer; the base coat is the primer. See the Finish Guide page for examples.

How are the miniatures prepared before shipping?

Models arrive fully assembled, with fixed turrets on vehicles. The resin prints are washed and fully cured, and supports are normally removed. Occasionally, a small support may be left in place to help stabilize a delicate part during shipping. It can be carefully removed after delivery with tweezers or a hobby knife. Further cleanup is generally not needed.

What do the Mike Lambo Full Packs replace?

Each Full Pack includes miniature replacements for all model counters in its named game. State and status counters, such as loaded, crew, and turret-damage markers, are not included. The packs work with all currently available editions.

Are sets separate from individual tanks?

Yes. Sets have their own browse page and detail pages, and each set page lists exactly what is included.

What if I need a tank that is not listed yet?

Use the Requests page and send me the vehicle name, plus any useful details such as scale, finish, or references.

Are resin-printed tanks fragile?

MiniTankForge models use a custom tougher resin blend chosen to reduce brittleness and give vulnerable parts such as long barrels a little flex. They are still detailed miniatures rather than toys, but they are designed for normal tabletop handling, storage, and transport. Watch the real drop and barrel-flex test.
