Prototype and What-if Tank Miniatures

Browse unusual tanks that were prototypes, unfinished projects, or paper designs rather than normal production vehicles.

These models are best for alternate-history games, prototype displays, collection variety, or scenario pieces where unusual armor is part of the fun.

What This Page Groups

The main tank catalog still keeps chronological era separate from vehicle status. A tank can be WW2-era and still be a paper project, or Cold War-era and still be a prototype.

How To Use Them

Use prototype and what-if vehicles as centerpiece models, special objectives, or small themed groups. For regular historical forces, mix them carefully with service vehicles.
