Core US Vehicles

Sherman-based forces are the natural center of many US WW2 collections. Tank destroyers such as the M10 Wolverine and M18 Hellcat add speed and anti-armor roles, while Stuart, Greyhound, Priest, Lee, and half-track models fill out support and scenario needs.

Good First Picks

Start with Sherman M4A3, M18 Hellcat, M10 Wolverine, and M5A1 Stuart. Add M7 Priest, M8 Greyhound, Pershing, or M3 Half-track when you want more variety than a pure tank lineup.
