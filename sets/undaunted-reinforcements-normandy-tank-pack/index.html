Tank pieces for Undaunted: Reinforcements

This set is currently in development. It is planned as an unofficial 1:250 tank-only accessory pack for Undaunted: Reinforcements players who want physical vehicles in place of the Normandy vehicle counters. The planned mix is two Shermans, one Stuart, two Panzer IVs, and one Panther, but final details may still change before release.

Scale note

Fixed 1:250 game scale

Fixed at 1:250 so the tanks stay readable on Undaunted map tiles without taking over the tile space. This pack is not a variable-scale starter set.
