WW2 Tank Destroyer Miniatures

Compare tank destroyers across German, Soviet, and American forces for tabletop anti-armor roles.

Tank destroyers work well as focused support pieces, scenario threats, or themed groups beside medium and heavy tanks.

How This Role Feels

Tank destroyers give a force a different silhouette from standard tanks. German casemate vehicles, Soviet SU and ISU types, and American turreted destroyers each fit different tabletop themes.

Set Options

German and Soviet tank destroyer sets are good shortcuts when you want a themed group. American destroyers work well beside Sherman and US starter packs.
